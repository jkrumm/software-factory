# Title of the artifact

> **TLDR.** What is proposed, why, and what you are asked to decide, in three sentences at most.

## Decision

The one thing that needs a yes, a no or a change. Say what happens without an answer.

## Options

|Option|Cost|Reversible|
|-|-|-|
|Recommended option|Time, risk, money|Yes|
|Alternative|Time, risk, money|No|

Recommendation: the option, and the one reason it wins.

## How it works

The shape of the change in a few short paragraphs or a list. Name the parts by what they do.

- First step
- Second step

```
a command or a snippet worth reading
```

## Risks

What can go wrong and how it would show. Say which risk you would not take.

## Proof

What was run and what it showed. For a Report, this is the section to read first: what changed, what was verified, what is left.

## Open questions

- What you could not settle and who can.
