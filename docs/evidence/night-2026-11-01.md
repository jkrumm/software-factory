- **Defect 1: one `pgrep -f` ended every factory session at 20:12; about 2.5 h lost (resumed 22:49).** The Athena worker `prometheus-epx-w1` ran
  `for p in $(pgrep -f gate.sh); do pkill -TERM -P $p; kill $p; done`. `pgrep -f` matches the whole argv, and the factory starts Claude with the prompt as its last
  argument (`docs/design.md`, the runner), so every session whose brief named `gate.sh` matched: SIGTERM ended eight sessions in one second. Postgres (Colima) was also down until
  20:25, so the factory was out 20:12 to 20:25; the machine did not reboot. The restart rule did not cover it: the core did not restart, so no `runner_restarted`, and
  rotation 5 resumed the lead and its two workers by hand, under their old names (the lead's terminal `t41` → `t4h`). PR #19 had finished before. Fix, live: dotfiles `1942aec`, `hooks/broad-kill-guard.ts`, a PreToolUse guard
  in all three account settings that denies `pkill -f`, `killall` and kills fed by `pgrep -f` (known limits in its header; by rotation 5). Open beyond the guard: a prompt in argv makes every process-table match hit briefs; sessions
  killed without a core restart have no automatic resume.
- **Decision 7 has no lever.** Admission picks the open account with the lowest week and takes `me` at 92 % (it closes at 95 %),
