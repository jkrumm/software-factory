Task:
The wake rule (PR #9, `rules.ts` `wake`) types `workers done: …` into a session waiting `--on workers`, keyed `wake:<session>:<wait event key>`. The wait itself stays open: only `factory session wait --done` records `wait_cleared` (`sessions.ts`). So after a wake the session's wait is spent but open: send-back (FL5) sees an open wait and lets every Stop through, and the session reads `waiting` again when the woken turn ends. Every orchestrator would have to remember `--done` after each wake. Found by the dotfiles retirement worker (dotfiles PR #53).

Fix: the wake ends the wait. When the wake note is delivered (a turn proves it, as for every note), the factory records a `wait_cleared` for that session in the same transaction, unless a newer `wait_declared` came after the one the wake answers (then the newer wait stays open). Decide whether the clear belongs at note creation or at proven delivery, and say why in the commit: delivery is the stronger reading (an undelivered wake must not clear the wait), but check what the session's derived state shows between the two. Tests through `evaluateRules` and the note delivery path: after a delivered wake the session is not `waiting` and a Stop with live children is sent back again; a wait declared after the wake's wait stays open; an undelivered wake clears nothing. Docs: the wake rule's paragraph in `AGENTS.md`, the `waiting` state's definition, §3's table in `docs/design.md`.
