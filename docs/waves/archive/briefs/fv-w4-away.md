# Brief: FV Wave 4, away and back

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/waves/fv.md` (Wave 4), `docs/research/2026-11-01-fleet-view.md` ("Away and back"), `docs/design.md` §11 "The owner's line" and "The fleet view" (Waves 1 to 3 as built), and `packages/server/src/fleet/` and `notify/` first. `agw digest` already has the `window` kind (`{id, paragraph}`, at most 60 words from the facts given, text at most 6 000 characters; agent-workflows `packages/host/src/workflows/digest/README.md`): no agw change.

1. **Presence, from human acts only.** `POST /fleet/presence {place: desk|phone, act}` (token only, like `/fleet`); `act` is a short enum (`focus`, `looked`, `board_click`, `unlock`, `memo`, `foreground`, `caught_up`), `memo` and `foreground` only for `phone`. Each act is a row (place, act, at; kept 30 days). Derived, never stored: the desk is present while its last act is under 45 min old, the phone under 10 min; `away` is neither. `/fleet` gains `presence { desk: {at, present}, phone: {at, present}, away, awaySince, seenAt }`. Nothing else moves presence: no hook event, prompt, note, `rd say` or HID idle.
2. **`seen_at`, one owner-wide cursor, server-side.** It moves to now on `caught_up` (any place) and on an act (`POST /fleet/asks/:id/act`, which now records a presence act for the place it names, default `desk`) when that act leaves no other ask of the open window in Needs you. Never backwards.
3. **`GET /fleet/since?t=<iso>`** (token only; `t` defaults to `seen_at`, at most 7 days back, a later or malformed `t` is 400), from recorded facts only, grouped:
   - *Needs you*: asks with `at` ≥ t, `yours` first, with tier, suggestion and actions as `/fleet` has them.
   - *Shipped*: Issues closed shipped in the window; commits on each repo's default branch (first-parent, the snapshot builder keeps the last 20 per repo with sha, subject, at; git stays off the request path and off the tick, bounded as today); plan waves with a dated `done` in the window.
   - *Went wrong, then recovered*: a failure followed by its fix in the window, one collapsed line each: a spawn that failed attempts and then started; an effect or notification that failed and then succeeded; a digest run that failed and then ran.
   - *Handled for you*: `fleet_triage_log` rows of the window, each with its undo id while not undone, plus the owner's own acts.
   - *Still running*: sessions alive now, with headline and how long they have run.
   - *Spend*: per account, the weekly share at the window's start (the nearest `quota_readings` row at or before t) and now; the digest's own spend in the window.
   Every line carries its source (table and key, or repo and sha) so the gate can trace it.
4. **The headline paragraph** is the only model text: one `agw digest` call of kind `window` per distinct window, input the groups' **non-WorkRoot** facts only (an WorkRoot item is a count, never its text: `scopeOf` decides), cached by the facts' hash. `GET /fleet/since` never waits on agw: it answers `headline {state: ready|pending|failed|none, text}` and a missing one is queued for the digest pass (the Wave 2 lifecycle: one run in flight, a backoff, a daily cap). The first desk act after an away window queues its window at once, so the board's card has its paragraph by the time it is read.
5. **The away rule, in the outbox.** At send time: `notify:ask:*` (a decision, the `yours` tier) goes out unless the desk is present; `notify:digest:*` and `notify:test:*` always go out; every other message goes out only while the phone is present and the desk is not, and otherwise is `folded` (a new status, with the reason, never sent later: its facts are in the since reading). Before the board reports presence the owner is always away, so only decisions and the morning reading reach Slack: intended.
6. **The morning reading.** The 07:30 message becomes the since reading's groups for the window from `seen_at` (at most 24 h back) as plain text, under 3 000 characters, with the existing cuts and scrubbing (`notify/text.ts`). **No model text in it** (SF2: a message is a template filled with facts, so the paragraph stays on the board), and an WorkRoot item is a count only: nothing of WorkRoot leaves the mini.
7. `factory fleet presence <place> <act>`, `factory fleet since [--t <iso>]`, `factory fleet seen` (caught up); `--format json|plain`; token only (exit 3 without).
8.
