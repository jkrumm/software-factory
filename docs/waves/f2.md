# F2: the Issue machine

**Goal:** a request becomes an Issue in Postgres, a lead in its own worktree takes it from root cause to production,
and the Issue closes only on evidence the factory derived from what the lead's commits touched; a regression inside the
hold goes back to the same lead. `project.yaml` v0 says how each project ships. Decisions SF2, SF5, SF6 and the TDD
carry-overs in `docs/decisions.md`; design `docs/design.md` §4 (the Issue machine), §5 (the loop, the lead's job, done
means evidence, the brief, following a lead), §9 (projects, v0 only), §12 (the F2 tables).
**Gate:** per wave, `make check` green on the merged head, `/review` clean or every finding answered (plus one pass on
the fix commits), landed with `rd merge`, `make deploy` and `make verify` live on the mini. The track's gate (Wave 6):
the two F2 gamedays of §13 and three real requests shipped end to end with nobody typing into a lead's pane.

**How this plan runs.** As F1: each wave is one `rd fan` worker (Sonnet, `roles/worker.md`'s brief template) in a
worktree of this repo, landed by the program lead with `rd merge`; a part in another repo (agw's classify workflow,
dotfiles' hooks) is a second worker on disjoint files. One wave at a time: the waves share `packages/server`. Briefs
live in `docs/waves/briefs/f2-w<n>-*.md`. Tooling: oxlint and oxfmt, `bun test`, no Effect, no model SDK (SF2).

**Out of scope here:** intake sources, dedup, settle and runbooks (F3); the orchestrator, initiatives and Issue-level
capacity (F4); Argo pages (A2); iris screens (IR). F2 takes requests from `factory issue new` and `POST /issues` only.

## Wave 3 — Classify and the lead            <!-- status: pending -->
- [ ] Classify (SF2: the factory never calls a model): one agw workflow (agent-workflows, its own worker) returning
  project, kind, class, size, the lead's tier and effort, with an eval set; the factory calls it like any client and
  stores the answer. A failed classify leaves the Issue `queued` with defaults, never stuck.
- [ ] Admission spawns a lead (role `lead`, tier and effort from Classify within the role table's bounds) in a worktree
  `factory/<issue>-<attempt>` made the way `rd fan` makes one, pane `lead-<issue>-<attempt>`; spawning again attaches.
- [ ] The brief (§5 "The brief"): the ask, facts as data, history marked *past, may not apply* (empty until F3), the
  project from its manifest, done means, the standing orders identical in every brief. Prompt id
  `issue:<id>:<phase>:<n>` recorded before typing.
- [ ] The lead's verbs: `factory issue phase|wait|ship|close|block|ask|drop`, each an inbox row with its key. `ask`
  records a `decisions` row; how it reaches the owner is IR's (until then, a line in `factory status`).
- [ ] The lead's send-back (§5 "Following a lead"): a lead that stops with nothing reported since its last prompt, no
  wait and no limit is sent back with one line; a second unreported stop parks the Issue with a line for the owner.

## Wave 4 — Evidence, the hold and finalize            <!-- status: pending -->
- [ ] Required evidence derived from the merged commits' paths against the manifest (§5's table): `ship` refused without
  it, `close` refused when the commits touch deployable paths; the factory checks existence and freshness, never judges.
- [ ] `watching`: the hold (manifest `hold`, default 30 min), then `make verify` and the probes; green → closed, red →
  back to `working` with a note to the same lead, which stays open through the hold.
- [ ] Finalize once from every exit path: terminal state, claim released, tab closed, worktree removed, next promoted;
  the attempt's transcripts copied into the archive (stored and linked, never parsed); `factory issue resume` opens one
  with `--fork-session`.
- [ ] `closed → queued` for the same fault within 30 days (a request's reopen; F3 adds signals).
