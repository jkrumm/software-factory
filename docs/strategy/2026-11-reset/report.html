Software factory reset

Strategy draft · 2026-11-04 · built from eight read-only audits of the factory, dotfiles, brain, agw, Hermes, Iris, Argo and the live fleet, plus external research (DeepSeek super-agent, pr-agent, Claude Code docs, issue 97074)
Verdict. One item in the queue gets exactly one visible lead tab. The lead plans, delegates to subagents (Explore, implementer, reviewer), ships, deploys and verifies. The factory shrinks to a deterministic loop around that tab: queue, admission, hooks, asks, ship gate and one closer. Hidden sessions, a tab per wave, the review transport and the fleet-triage machinery go. Order: lean review → the lead foundation → orchestration and surfaces on top.

2. The model

Open architecture.html directly for pan, zoom, guided views and export.

The life of one item

Plan with you (planner tab, Opus)→decision doc reviewed in Argo→factory issue new --plan→admitted→lead tab opens in its worktree→build with subagents→static gate→review at the tier→merge, deploy→factory re-runs make verify→achieved; tab and worktree closed
  • One tab per item, no rotation. autoCompactWindow is already 600k; the plan file is the state, and a SessionStart(compact) hook re-injects "re-read the plan". Work that needs more than one context is split into child items, each with its own lead.
  • Questions. A lead files one structured question per item with factory issue ask; asking again replaces it. It appears in the Herdr sidebar and as an Iris push. Your answer, from Iris, the sidebar or the tab itself, goes to that lead. Follow-ups on a running item go straight to its lead, never through the planner (DeepSeek's "subscription pass-through").
  • Machine failures never reach you. They retry within their budget, then the item is parked with a reason on a health line. An ask older than 7 days expires to parked.
  • Done means verified. Merged, deployed, the factory's own make verify green, review covered at its tier. Only then is the item achieved and the factory, the single closer, kills the tab and removes the worktree.

3. Review: one agent, three settings

agw stays model-free for review: agw review route decides the tier from rules, prepare packs the diff with the plan's acceptance criteria and the static results, submit/dispose keep the ledger and covered proves it. The model runs as the lead's own reviewer subagent; the /review skill passes model and effort per call (Claude Code ≥ 2.1.292 supports both; installed 2.1.318).

TierWhen (rules, no model)WhoPasses
noneDocs, plans, lockfiles, tests only, generated files; or under ~40 added lines outside risky paths with the static gate green—Static gate only
lightNormal codereviewer · Sonnet medium1 pass, important findings only, nits capped at 3
deepAuth, secrets, migrations, deploy, hooks, admission, CI; or more than 600 lines; or a high revert floorreviewer · Opus high, then review-verifier · Sonnet medium per important finding1 pass + 1 verifier per finding (not a 3-vote)
  • Intent first. The reviewer answers two questions before any line comment: does the diff do something the plan did not ask for, and is something the plan asked for missing?
  • When. Once per branch at ship, not per wave. The wave gate's covered requirement goes; the factory's ship gate keeps it.
  • Static gate before the model. make check, fallow audit (blocks only what the change adds), gitleaks; ast-grep rules for AGENTS.md invariants later. Their results enter the prompt as "known, do not report".
  • Convergence. At most 2 rounds; round 2 sees only the fix delta and the open fingerprints (file + anchor text + claim). After that the lead records a disposition; you see only security-class findings.
  • Measured. Acted-on rate per tier from the dispositions, window share per review from usage-tracker. Thresholds move on those numbers only.
